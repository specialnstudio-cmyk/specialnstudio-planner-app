Backup & help

Swipe left or right to turn the page (arrow keys on a computer). Tap a square, circle or drop to colour it in. On note pages, + adds a new sheet; tap the page number to name your sheets and jump to one. On the vision board and the bookshelf, tap a frame or a book to add a photo. Your planner is saved on this device only. To move it to another phone, tablet or computer, save a backup here and restore it there.
